[Q: A2_Q_ENTITLE]

  2. I brought it up with the person involved
  3. I held onto it — it's still bothering me

  → I held onto it — it's still bothering me

[REFLECTION]
There's nothing wrong with wanting your effort to be seen. That's a real human need. The question worth sitting with is: was today's attention mostly on what you received, or on what you gave? One of those energises a team. The other, quietly, doesn't.

[BRIDGE]
One more lens. We've talked about your agency and your contribution. Now — how wide was your view today? Who else were you thinking about?

[Q: A3_OPEN]

[Q: A3_Q_NARROW]
You were zoomed in on your own problem today — which is often necessary. But let's look around the edges: was there anyone near you who might have been struggling too, even if you didn't engage with it?
  1. Yes, I noticed — I just didn't have capacity
  2. Yes, and I realise I could have checked in
  3. Not really — I was heads-down
  4. I hadn't thought about it until now

  → I hadn't thought about it until now

[REFLECTION]
You were in your own world today — and sometimes that's exactly where you need to be. But meaning at work rarely lives there permanently. Tomorrow, try one small thing: notice one person's experience before your day ends. Not to fix it. Just to see it.

A tough day on every dimension. External, expecting, inward-facing. Be honest with yourself about whether this is a rough patch or a pattern.

None of this is a verdict. It's a snapshot. The most useful question isn't 'was I good today?' — it's 'what am I noticing?'
